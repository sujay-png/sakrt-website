Sustainable Bag Materials Used in Organic Bag Manufacturing

A real sustainable bag begins with the materials used, not just the final product. SAKRT makes compostable bags from plant-based materials like corn starch. These materials are turned into small pieces, then melted and formed into a film.The film is cut and sealed to make the bag. This process lets the bag break down in compost along with food waste, instead of ending up in a landfill for a long time.

Every bag made by SAKRT is made to decompose within a certain time in a composting environment. After breaking down, it leaves only water, carbon dioxide, and soil-like material. This is checked by outside labs, not just said by the company itself.

FAQs

1. What certification should a genuine organic bag carry?

A genuine organic bag should carry IS 17088 certification, which confirms the material breaks down into compost within a defined period without leaving toxic residue.

2. Is there a minimum order quantity for organic bags wholesale?

Minimum order quantities vary by bag size and thickness, and SAKRT shares this detail once business requirements are shared during the inquiry stage.

3. How is an organic bag different from a regular biodegradable bag?

An organic bag is compostable and turns into usable organic matter under controlled conditions, while a biodegradable bag may only fragment into smaller plastic-like pieces without fully composting.

4. Can SAKRT supply custom-printed retail bags for my brand?

Yes, SAKRT offers custom printing on retail bags and carry bags for businesses that want their branding included on compostable packaging.

5. How long does bulk delivery take after placing an order?

Delivery timelines depend on order volume and are confirmed at the time of quotation, with sample approval usually completed before full production begins.
