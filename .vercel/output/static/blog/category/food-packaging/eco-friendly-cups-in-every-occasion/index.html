Eco-friendly cups That Can Handle Both Hot and Cold Drinks

A lot of people wonder: Eco-friendly cups really work for both hot and cold drinks? The short answer is definitely yes. Today’s compostable cups are made from smart, biodegradable materials that stay strong even when the temperature changes. Whether you’re serving a hot cup of coffee or a cold glass of lemonade, these cups won’t bend, leak, or change shape.
