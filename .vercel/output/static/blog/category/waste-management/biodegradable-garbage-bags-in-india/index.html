Waste management with biodegradable garbage bags

India’s waste management system is changing quickly, with biodegradable garbage bags leading the way in this shift. But new rules like the Plastic Waste Management Rules, 2016, have pushed for greener options. We make these eco-friendly bags using materials from plants, such as cornstarch and cellulose. The Environmental Impact of Adopting Biodegradable Solutions

The impact of regular garbage bags on the environment is huge—plastics can take hundreds of years to break down, and during that time, they release tiny plastic particles into the soil and water. On the other hand, biodegradable garbage bags are made to break down naturally and safely in the environment. Our products are approved by standards like ASTM D6400, which means they fully decompose in industrial composting plants. Picture this: your kitchen waste, neatly wrapped in a biodegradable bag, helps make the soil in your local community garden richer instead of ending up in a faraway landfill. This idea of renewal is at the heart of how we make our products.

Additionally, by using less plastic made from fossil fuels, these bags greatly reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Research shows that switching to biodegradable bags can cut a household’s plastic waste by up to 80%. In India, where waste production in cities is expected to hit 165 million tons by 2030, these changes are especially important. Our work in distributing these bags helps more people make eco-friendly choices and promotes wider use of sustainable options.
