Why Compostable Courier bag?

Compostable courier bag are the future of responsible packaging. Unlike traditional plastic mailers that pollute land and oceans for hundreds of years, compostable mailer bags break down naturally within months. They help businesses reduce their environmental footprint while staying compliant with Indian plastic regulations. Choosing compostable courier bag reflects your brand's commitment to sustainability, builds trust with eco-conscious customers, and contributes to a cleaner, greener planet without compromising on strength, safety, or performance.
